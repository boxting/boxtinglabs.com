# Brag Plan: Boxting Labs

## What is this app?
A boutique software, applied-AI and infrastructure studio in Lima whose site promises software built around how your operation already works.

## The angle
The slogan is the product. The headline is typed out live, like someone writing it, while the studio's own "what we build" cards (Agente IA, Servidor, Web) cycle beside it and do their thing. It's a 5-second, GIF-style loop for the LinkedIn feed, with no title card and no outro.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A caret blinks, then "El software se adapta a tu proceso." types itself out in Instrument Serif. Meanwhile the Agente IA card on the right is already answering a chat.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The headline being written character by character.
- "No al revés." lands in orange italic, the payoff.
- The feature cards rotate: the agent replies, server health bars fill to a green check, and a cursor clicks a web CTA that makes result cards rise.

## Outro / punchline
No outro. The text eases out over the last 0.4s and the card stack returns to its first state, so the loop is seamless.

## User flow worth showing
None, because this is a landing page only. It relies on the hero's "Lo que construimos" frames (`public/svg/frames/*`).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium GIF loop, user-specified: 5s, no intro title
- Interpretation: one scene, one idea, restrained motion, the long hold on the full phrase is the point.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 5s (user override of the 15–25s default)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #08090B with orange/sky/plum ambient glows (hero AmbientField / OG image)
- Accent: #FE5D1C → #FF8D5C gradient
- Text: #F8F3EB, muted #8A92A1
- Display font: Instrument Serif (regular + italic)
- Body font: DM Sans; labels DM Mono
- Strongest visual element: the frame cards from the hero carousel

## Share copy (draft)
El software se adapta a tu proceso. No al revés. Software, IA aplicada e infraestructura a la medida de cómo ya trabaja tu operación.

## Audio direction
- Role: intentional silence. It's a GIF-style loop and LinkedIn autoplays muted.
- Music: none · SFX: none

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Loop — 5.0s
0.0–0.3 caret blinks. 0.3–1.9 "El software se adapta a tu proceso." types out. 2.05 "No al revés." slides up in orange italic. 2.3–4.55 holds the full phrase (9 words, ~2.7s of read time from first word). 4.55–5.0 the text fades out for the loop.
Right side: a card stack. Agente IA is in focus 0–1.6 (chat bubbles pop in), Servidor 1.6–3.2 (bars fill, check), Web 3.2–4.8 (cursor click, result cards rise), then it returns to Agente IA by 5.0.
Persistent: a small logo lockup top-left and "boxtinglabs.com" bottom-left. They aren't animated, so the loop doesn't jump.
Sequential/interaction: yes, the typed headline and the simulated chat, health check, and cursor click.
Audio: none.
